About HopeTrack

Built around evidence, dignity, and local partnership.

HopeTrack International is a fictional nonprofit created for a portfolio case study, but its operating logic mirrors real-world education campaigns: measurable goals, strong field storytelling, and data-informed decisions across fundraising and program delivery.

Mission statement

We connect donors, campaign teams, and country programs around one shared commitment: helping children stay in school and thrive in safe learning environments.

Timeline

Key milestones from 2020 to 2026.

Each milestone reflects a step toward sharper fundraising operations and stronger education outcomes.

2020

HopeTrack launches with a child-centered education mission.

The organization begins as a small coalition connecting education recovery and donor-funded school support.

2022

First regional campaign proves the value of better digital tracking.

Leadership identifies attribution gaps and starts investing in stronger channel-level measurement.

2024

Program expansion reaches East Africa and South Asia.

HopeTrack pilots education access and digital skills programming in multiple field contexts.

2026

Education for All becomes the flagship integrated fundraising campaign.

The organization aligns donor messaging, web journeys, and campaign analytics to raise $50,000 and recruit 200 new members.
